Straight answers

Everything you're quietly wondering.

And if yours isn't here, ask it in the group — it'll get a straight answer too.

Do I really need zero coding experience?

Yes — zero means zero. If you can open a browser and type, you can build along. Every step is explained live, and the AI tools do the heavy lifting. You'll be surprised how fast “zero” turns into “wait, I made that.”

I'm from mechanical / civil / commerce — is this for me?

Especially for you. AI is changing entry-level work in every field, not just software. The sessions assume no background at all — if anything, the most interesting builds come from people solving problems outside of tech.

What if I fall behind during the live build?

That's what “live” is for. Ask in the moment and Avi stays on the step until no one is stuck. You're building with a guide, not watching a video — falling behind just means someone pauses for you.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The Zoom sessions, the WhatsApp group, the café meetups — all free. The only fee is showing up.

What do I need to bring?

A laptop, a decent internet connection, and two hours on a Sunday evening. The Zoom link comes from the WhatsApp group — that's the whole setup.

What if I miss a Sunday?

Nothing breaks. Stamps never expire and every week is a fresh problem — rejoin whenever you can, and keep filling your passport at your own pace.
